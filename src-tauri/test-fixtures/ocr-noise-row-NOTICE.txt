Regression fixture for Tesseract row-noise rejection.

Source: SAACT 6, The Neo-Assyrian Myth of Ishtar's Descent and Resurrection
(2017), source PDF page 49, line 126 C: an opening bracket and six dots.

Prepared with the saved Eduba page settings (300 DPI extraction, 0.5 degree
deskew with expansion). Crop in processed page pixels: 390 568 650 637.
Existing OCR words are masked and 20 pixels of white padding are added on
each side, using prepareRegionOcrImage. No text recognition result is edited.

With the bundled model, PSM 6 and PSM 7 return no words with ordinary row
noise rejection, and recover the bracket and dots with textord_noise_rejrows=0.
This fixture contains only the missing punctuation, not the full page.
